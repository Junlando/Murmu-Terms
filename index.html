Murmu Terms of Use

一、服務內容

Murmu 是一款讓使用者自由表達當下心情、思緒與生活片段的 App。你可以透過文字、emoji、心情圖示等方式，將這些 Murmur 分享給你的親密朋友。

二、隱私與分享

Murmu 鼓勵用戶以更輕鬆、無壓力的方式分享情緒與想法。所有內容僅限您指定的好友查看，平台不會對外公開您的 Murmur。

三、使用者行為

請尊重其他使用者，不得發布違法、攻擊性、歧視或侵害他人權益之內容。

四、資料儲存

您的 murmur 將儲存在雲端，並透過 Firebase 或等效服務提供同步與備份。資料安全請參閱 Firebase 服務條款。

五、帳號與訂閱方案

本 App 提供免費使用方案，亦提供進階訂閱服務。

  • 訂閱用戶可享有更多 emoji 圖片套組、心情分析等額外功能。
  • 訂閱採自動續訂制，將依您選擇的方案定期自動收費（如每月、每年）。
  • 您可隨時於帳號設定中取消續訂，取消後仍可於當期訂閱期間內繼續使用服務。
  • 所有付款均由平台帳戶（如 Apple ID、Google Play）處理。
  • 退款規定依平台（Apple、Google）政策為準。

六、其他

若有任何疑問，請透過 App 中聯絡我們的方式與我們聯繫。

1. Service Overview

Murmu is an app that lets users casually share thoughts, feelings, or daily moments. You can express yourself through text, emojis, and mood icons, and share your murmurs with close friends only.

2. Privacy & Sharing

Murmu promotes a low-pressure way to express yourself. All murmurs are visible only to your selected friends. Murmu does not publish your content publicly.

3. User Conduct

Please respect others. Do not post illegal, offensive, discriminatory, or harmful content.

4. Data Storage

Your murmurs are stored securely via cloud services such as Firebase. For details, refer to Firebase's terms of service.

5. Account & Subscription

  • Murmu is free to use, with optional premium subscriptions.
  • Subscribers get access to more emoji packs, mood analysis, and bonus features.
  • Subscriptions auto-renew based on your selected plan (e.g., monthly or annually).
  • You can cancel anytime via your account settings. Canceled plans remain active until the end of the billing period.
  • All payments are processed via your platform account (e.g., Apple ID, Google Play).
  • Refunds follow the App Store or Play Store refund policy. Murmu does not handle direct refunds.

6. Contact

If you have any questions, please contact us through the support option in the app.
